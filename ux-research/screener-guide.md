# Weekndr — Participant Screener Guide
*Used for recruiting interview participants | Estimated completion: 5–7 min*

---

## Recruiter Notes
- Target **8–12 participants** for a generative discovery round (aim for saturation by session 8).
- Recruit across at least **3 cities** to avoid hyper-local activity bias.
- Aim for mix on: number of kids, age range of kids, household income, single vs. two-parent household.
- Disqualify gracefully — never tell a rejected candidate why they didn't qualify.
- All questions marked **(DQ)** are disqualifiers if answered as noted.

---

## Target Mix

| Dimension | Target Split |
|---|---|
| Kids aged 2–5 (toddler/preschool) | ~30% |
| Kids aged 6–10 (elementary) | ~40% |
| Kids aged 11–14 (tween) | ~30% |
| Single-parent households | ≥ 2 participants |
| Dual-income households | ≥ 4 participants |
| HHI < $75K | ≥ 2 participants |
| HHI $75K–$150K | ≥ 4 participants |
| HHI > $150K | ≥ 2 participants |
| Suburban location | ≥ 3 participants |
| Urban location | ≥ 3 participants |

## Screener Questions

### Part A — Basic Eligibility

**A1.** How many children do you have living at home?
- [ ] None *(DQ)*
- [ ] 1
- [ ] 2
- [ ] 3 or more

**A2.** What are the ages of your children? *(select all that apply)*
- [ ] Under 2 years old
- [ ] 2–5 years old
- [ ] 6–10 years old
- [ ] 11–14 years old
- [ ] 15 or older

> **DQ if:** all children are under 2 OR all children are 15 or older — outside the activity-planning sweet spot.

**A3.** How often does your family do an outing or activity outside the home on weekends? (e.g., parks, museums, events, day trips)
- [ ] Rarely or never *(DQ)*
- [ ] Once or twice a month
- [ ] Most weekends
- [ ] Almost every weekend

**A4.** Who in your household is primarily responsible for planning weekend activities for the kids?
- [ ] Mostly me
- [ ] Shared equally with a partner
- [ ] Mostly my partner *(DQ — need the decision maker)*
- [ ] Someone else (grandparent, nanny, etc.) *(DQ)*

---

### Part B — Planning Behavior

**B1.** How do you currently find or decide on weekend activities for your family? *(select all that apply)*
- [ ] Google search
- [ ] Apps (Yelp, Eventbrite, local apps)
- [ ] Social media (Instagram, Facebook groups, NextDoor)
- [ ] Word of mouth / asking friends
- [ ] I already have a set rotation of go-to places
- [ ] My kids suggest things
- [ ] Other: ___________

> *No DQ — used to understand planning behavior. Flag if "set rotation only" — lower pain signal.*

**B2.** How far in advance do you typically start planning what to do on the weekend?
- [ ] Day of
- [ ] The night before
- [ ] A few days ahead
- [ ] A week or more in advance

**B3.** How often do your weekend plans change or fall apart due to unexpected factors (weather, cost, kids' moods, etc.)?
- [ ] Never
- [ ] Rarely
- [ ] Sometimes
- [ ] Often

> *Flag "Never" — may indicate low pain. Prioritize "Sometimes" and "Often."*

**B4.** On a scale of 1–5, how stressful or time-consuming do you find weekend activity planning?
*(1 = not at all stressful, 5 = very stressful)*
- [ ] 1
- [ ] 2
- [ ] 3
- [ ] 4
- [ ] 5

> **DQ if 1.** Prioritize 3–5 for highest signal. Include at least 1–2 participants at score 2 for contrast.

---

### Part C — Tech & AI Comfort

**C1.** Do you currently use any apps or digital tools to help with family planning, scheduling, or activity discovery? *(select all that apply)*
- [ ] Google Calendar / Apple Calendar
- [ ] Family scheduling apps (Cozi, FamilyWall, etc.)
- [ ] Activity discovery apps (Yelp, Eventbrite, Meetup, etc.)
- [ ] AI assistants (ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Siri, etc.)
- [ ] None of the above

**C2.** Have you ever used an AI tool (like ChatGPT or Google Gemini) to help plan a trip, outing, or activity?
- [ ] Yes, regularly
- [ ] Yes, once or twice
- [ ] No, but I'd be open to it
- [ ] No, and I wouldn't be interested *(DQ — not open to AI-assisted planning)*

**C3.** How comfortable are you using new apps on your phone?
- [ ] Very comfortable — I try new apps often
- [ ] Somewhat comfortable — I'll try something if it seems worth it
- [ ] Not very comfortable — I stick to apps I know *(flag — may struggle with concept test)*
- [ ] Not comfortable at all *(DQ)*

---

### Part D — Disqualifying Conflicts

**D1.** Do you or does anyone in your household work for any of the following? *(select all that apply)*
- [ ] A travel or tourism company
- [ ] An event planning or ticketing platform
- [ ] A family activity or children's entertainment company
- [ ] A technology company building consumer apps
- [ ] A market research or UX research firm
- [ ] None of the above *(qualify)*

> **DQ if any box other than "None of the above" is checked.**

**D2.** Have you participated in a paid research study or product interview in the past 30 days?
- [ ] Yes *(DQ — research fatigue)*
- [ ] No *(qualify)*

---

### Part E — Logistics & Consent

**E1.** Are you available for a 45–60 minute video call in the next two weeks?
- [ ] Yes
- [ ] No *(DQ — scheduling)*

**E2.** This session will be recorded for internal research purposes only and will not be shared publicly. Do you consent to being recorded?
- [ ] Yes *(qualify)*
- [ ] No *(DQ)*

**E3.** What is your preferred availability? *(select all that apply)*
- [ ] Weekday mornings (9am–12pm)
- [ ] Weekday afternoons (12pm–5pm)
- [ ] Weekday evenings (5pm–8pm)
- [ ] Weekend mornings
- [ ] Weekend afternoons

---

### Part F — Open-Ended (for recruiter calibration only — not scored)

**F1.** In one or two sentences, describe the last time you planned a weekend activity with your kids. What happened?

> *Use this to gut-check engagement level and communication style. Look for: specificity, emotion, recall. Vague or one-word answers signal low engagement — deprioritize.*

---

## Qualification Summary

| Criteria | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Children aged 2–14 at home | At least one |
| Weekend outing frequency | Once/month or more |
| Planning decision maker | Primary or co-primary |
| Planning stress score | 2–5 (prioritize 3–5) |
| Open to AI-assisted planning | Yes or open to it |
| No conflict of interest | Confirmed |
| Available for 45–60 min video call | Confirmed |
| Consents to recording | Yes |

---

## Incentive
Recommended: **$75–$100 Amazon gift card** or equivalent for a 45–60 min session.
- Higher end ($100) for single parents, shift workers, or participants with scheduling constraints.
- Confirm incentive fulfillment timeline upfront (same day or within 48 hrs of session).
